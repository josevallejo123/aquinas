Enrollment terms

Last updated October 22, 2026

These terms apply when you enroll your student in a Lighthouse Prep program. We've kept them short and in plain words. If anything is unclear, email us at support@thelighthouseprep.com.

What you're buying

Each program is a set number of hours of one-on-one coaching for one student, online, with the coaches themselves:

  • SAT Accelerator: 20 coaching hours of SAT preparation with Allan. $4,400, or $4,180 paid in full.
  • Flagship Program: 40 coaching hours with both coaches: SAT preparation with Allan, and essay review, interview preparation and career guidance with Jon, including one recorded mock interview with written feedback. $7,600, or $7,220 paid in full.
  • Premier Program: 50 coaching hours: everything in the Flagship Program, with three recorded mock interviews instead of one and an introduction to an industry professional in a field your student is exploring. $9,200, or $8,740 paid in full.

Every program includes the parent and student dashboard and the +100 point promise below. A program is for one student; for a second child, enroll again. Your student's hours are theirs to use within 12 months of their first session. Sessions are online and one-on-one, and you schedule them with the coaches.

Paying

  • Pay in full and save 5%, or pay in three monthly payments of $1,466.66 for the SAT Accelerator, $2,533.33 for the Flagship Program or $3,066.66 for the Premier Program.
  • The three payments are charged to the same payment method on the same day of three months in a row, the first on the day you enroll. Then they stop.
  • Your program continues while the payments are made. If a payment doesn't go through, Stripe emails you and tries again, and sessions pause until it's paid.
  • A payment from a bank account can take up to four business days to clear. Your portal invitation follows once it does.
  • Stripe, our payment processor, handles every payment, so we never see your card number.

The +100 point promise

Every program includes it. If your student completes the full plan and improves by fewer than 100 points from the diagnostic to an official SAT, we add 8 hours of tutoring at no cost.

Changing your mind

If you decide after the first two sessions that we aren't the right fit, tell us before the third and we'll refund everything you've paid beyond those two sessions, at the hourly rate you paid. That rate is your program's price divided by its hours: $220 an hour for the SAT Accelerator, $190 for the Flagship Program and $184 for the Premier Program, or 5% less if you paid in full. If you chose three payments, any still to come are cancelled.

To ask, email support@thelighthouseprep.com from the address you enrolled with.

Sessions and recordings

A session is recorded only with the parent's and the student's consent, so the coaches can review it. Recordings aren't shared with anyone outside Lighthouse Prep.

Your student's account

Our programs are for students 13 and older, with a parent's consent. Your family's portal account belongs to you, the parent, and your student can have their own login with your consent. Our privacy policy covers the rest: what we keep, for how long, and how to ask us to delete it.

Contact

Questions about these terms or your enrollment go to support@thelighthouseprep.com.
