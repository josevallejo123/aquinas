Scores and scholarships

From 1200 to 1500, scholarships rise by an average of $19,200.

Many universities publish automatic merit awards tied to SAT scores. We averaged the four-year awards at 11 nationally ranked universities for a student with a 3.8 GPA, from 1200 to 1500.

Scores and earnings

Ten years out, students from higher-SAT colleges earned about $28,000 more a year.

Students who started at colleges where admitted students average 1400 or higher on the SAT had median earnings of about $83,000 ten years later. At colleges averaging 1100 to 1199, the median was about $54,500.

Much of that gap reflects who each college admits, not just what it adds. That is part of why a student's own score matters.

Among applicants to the most selective colleges, those with higher SAT or ACT scores went on to earn more and were more likely to reach top graduate schools and firms, even after accounting for which college they attended.

Chetty, Deming and Friedman, a 2026 study linking test scores to tax records

How we divide the work

One coach handles the test and the other handles the application.
