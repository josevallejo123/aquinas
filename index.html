Scores and scholarships

120 SAT points can be worth $72,000 in scholarships.Source 1

Scores and earnings

Ten years out, students from higher-SAT colleges earned about $28,000 more a year.Source 4

Students who started at colleges where admitted students average 1400 or higher on the SAT had median earnings of about $83,000 ten years later. At colleges averaging 1100 to 1199, the median was about $54,500.Source 4

Much of that gap reflects who each college admits, not just what it adds.Source 5 That is part of why a student's own score matters.

Among applicants to the most selective colleges, those with higher SAT or ACT scores went on to earn more and were more likely to reach top graduate schools and firms, even after accounting for which college they attended.

How we divide the work

One coach handles the test and the other handles the application.

Many families end up hiring a test tutor and an admissions consultant who never speak to each other. At Lighthouse Prep, Allan and Jon share one plan for your student and talk about their progress every week.

  • Full-length practice tests broken down by question type and time spent on each question
