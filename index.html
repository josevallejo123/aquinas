The founders

You work directly with the two of us, not a rotating bench of tutors.

A

Allan

Co-founder, SAT lead

Allan is an education professional who has spent more than a decade preparing students for the SAT. He teaches strategy as seriously as content: how to pace each module, which questions to skip, and how to guess well when time runs short.

His students review real test questions every week, because that is where most of the improvement happens. He is known for showing students what their best section would mean across a full test, which gives them a realistic target to work toward.

Our standards

What families can count on from us.

1The student's own words

We never write essays or interview answers. We help students find what they want to say and give honest feedback on their drafts.

2Honest claims

We do not guarantee admission, and any results we publish come with a clear explanation of how they were measured.

3Prices in the open

Every program price is listed on this page. Discounts are rare and always stated in writing.

4Founders do the work

Allan and Jon teach every session themselves. Your student is never handed off to someone you have not met.

5Parents stay in control

Accounts belong to parents, sessions are recorded only with consent, and there are no public profiles or student feeds.

6Real practice material

We teach from our own question sets and official practice tests, and we keep College Board content out of every AI tool we use.
